Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Datamous collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website (datamous.github.io), subscribe to the Datamous newsletter, download our free resources, or contact us. Datamous is a publishing brand that creates books and learning resources about data careers.

We keep this simple: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your information, and you can leave at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address. When you subscribe to the newsletter or request a free resource.
  • Messages you send us. If you reply to an email or write to us, we receive your message and the email address it came from.
  • Basic email statistics. Our newsletter provider may record when you subscribed and whether you opened or clicked an email.

We do not collect payment details. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number or any sensitive information.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the free resource you asked for, such as our free cheat sheets.
  • To send you the Datamous newsletter: practical tips, data stories, and news about new or free Datamous books and resources.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To understand which emails are useful, so we can improve them.

We will never sell, rent or trade your email address, and we will not use it for any purpose other than those listed here.

3. Legal basis (EU and UK readers)

We process your information based on your consent, which you give when you subscribe. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing.

4. Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to run the newsletter and deliver our resources. They process data only as needed to provide their service, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts our website, datamous.github.io. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit, for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Kit (kit.com) runs our email list: when you sign up, your email address goes straight to Kit, which stores it and sends our emails. See the Kit Privacy Policy: https://kit.com/privacy

Our books are sold through online retailers such as Amazon. When you buy a book, the retailer handles your purchase and personal details under its own privacy policy. We do not receive the personal information of people who buy our books.

5. Cookies

We do not use cookies or tracking tools on datamous.github.io. Kit may record whether you open or click our emails, and may use cookies on its own pages, such as the page where you manage your subscription. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your email address for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information, we remove it within 30 days, except where we must keep a record by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. You can:

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the one-click link at the bottom of every email.
  • Contact us at thedatamous@gmail.com to make any other request. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU or UK and believe we have not handled your information correctly, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process information in the United States and other countries. They use appropriate safeguards to protect it.

9. Children

Our newsletter is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has subscribed, please contact us and we will remove their details.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If we make significant changes, we will let subscribers know by email.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at thedatamous@gmail.com.
